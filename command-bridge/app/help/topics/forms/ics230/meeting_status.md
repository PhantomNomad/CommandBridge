# Was this meeting held?

This page records whether an [ICS 230](help:forms/ics230/index) meeting took place. You get here from the "Was this meeting held?" reminder on the dashboard, or from the **Was it held?** button on the ICS 230.

- **Completed (meeting was held)**: the meeting happened. It then shows as "Meeting held: ..." on everyone's [ICS 214](help:forms/ics214/index) for that operational period, and the ECC display scrolls past it.
- **Cancelled**: it didn't happen and won't. It no longer shows as upcoming.

You can add a short note, for example the actual start time or why it was cancelled. The note is added to the meeting's notes and the change goes in the [audit log](help:audit/index).

This works even after the ICS 230 has been signed, and for meetings in an earlier operational period. The meeting chair, the Incident Commander (or Unified Command), the Planning Section Chief, anyone who can edit the ICS 230 and a SysAdmin can use it. Once the status is set, the reminder disappears.
