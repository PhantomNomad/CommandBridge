# ICS 203 - Organization Assignment List

The ICS 203 shows which parts of the ICS organization are active and who is in each position. It is part of the Incident Action Plan (IAP) and is the source for the wall chart, the [ICS 207](help:forms/ics207/index).

- **Usually prepared by:** the Resources Unit, under the Planning Section Chief.
- **Who gets it:** everyone who receives the IAP. The signed original goes to the Documentation Unit.

General steps for signing and printing are in [Working with ICS forms](help:forms/working_with_forms).

## How it is filled in

You don't type on this form. Command Bridge builds it from the people **currently assigned** to incident positions. To change it, change the assignments in [Position assignments](help:operational_periods/assignments) and the 203 updates straight away.

- Only positions that are in use show a name. The rest are left blank on purpose, and you can't type into them here.
- The form shows as **Complete** once an Incident Commander is assigned.
- The table lists the section, the position and the person assigned.
- The **Position Assignments** button on the dashboard opens the assignments page.

## Guidance from the form

- Fill only the positions you are using. Small incidents need few positions; the organization grows and shrinks with the incident.
- A box can hold more than one name, for example Unified Commanders or deputies. They print in the same box, separated by commas.
- Show a trainee by adding "(T)" after the name, for example "A. Smith (T)". Do this on the person's assignment.
- Unified Commanders should show their agency.

## Signing and printing

When the organization is settled for the period, the Planning Section Chief normally approves it. **Preview PDF** prints the current assignments on the official form. Names that don't fit, and positions the official form has no box for (such as custom positions), are printed on a continuation sheet.
