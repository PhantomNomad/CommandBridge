# ICS 202 - Incident Objectives

The ICS 202 sets out what the incident will try to achieve in the next operational period: the objectives, where command wants the emphasis, and the main safety points. It is usually the cover page of the Incident Action Plan (IAP) and lists the other forms that make up the plan.

- **Usually prepared by:** the Planning Section, after the Command and General Staff meeting for the coming period.
- **Approved by:** the Incident Commander. Under Unified Command, one IC can approve it for all.
- **Who gets it:** everyone who receives the IAP, down to Section, Branch, Division/Group and Unit supervisors. The approved original goes to the Documentation Unit.

General steps for saving and printing are in [Working with ICS forms](help:forms/working_with_forms).

## What goes in each block

| Block | What to enter |
|---|---|
| 1. Incident Name | Filled in from the incident. |
| 2. Operational Period | Pick the period this plan is for. Its start and end dates and times print on the form. |
| 3. Objectives | Short, clear statements of what the response must achieve, most important first. Write them so each one is specific, measurable, starts with an action, can be done with the resources you have, and has a time frame. Include objectives for this period and for the incident as a whole. |
| 4. Operational Period Command Emphasis | Where command wants people to focus if they have to choose: tactical priorities, the order to tackle things, or warnings such as falling debris. It is not a restatement of the objectives. |
| 5. General Situational Awareness | Weather, how the incident is behaving, and any general safety message. If you put a safety message here, have the Safety Officer check that it agrees with the [ICS 208](help:forms/ics208/index). |
| Site Safety Plan | Tick if the Safety Officer says a site safety plan is needed, and say where the approved plan can be found. |
| 6. Incident Action Plan | Tick the forms that are part of this IAP. See below. |
| 7. Prepared By | Your name and ICS position. |
| 8. Approved By Incident Commander | Filled in when the IC approves. |

## IAP forms and approval

Command Bridge checks which IAP forms have been filled in for the selected operational period and ticks them for you, marked **(detected)**:

- [ICS 203](help:forms/ics203/index): an Incident Commander is assigned
- [ICS 204](help:forms/ics204/index), [ICS 205](help:forms/ics205/index), [ICS 206](help:forms/ics206/index) and [ICS 208](help:forms/ics208/index): the form has been saved for this period
- [ICS 205A](help:forms/ics205a/index): the communications list has entries

**The 202 can't be approved until all six are detected.** The form lists any that are missing.

Only the assigned Incident Commander or a SysAdmin can approve. Save the draft, then press **Approve / Submit ICS202**. The status changes to Approved and shows who approved it and when.

## Other attachments

Maps, weather forecasts, tide or current tables, a blank [ICS 214](help:forms/ics214/index) and any other documents that go with the plan can be uploaded under **Other Attachments** once the draft is saved (BMP, JPG, PNG, PDF or TXT, up to 15 MB each). They are listed on the form under Other Attachments and printed after it.
