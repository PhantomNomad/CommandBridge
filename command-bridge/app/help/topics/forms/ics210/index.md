# ICS 210 - Resource Status Change

The ICS 210 records changes in a resource's status as they are reported, for example a crew becoming available or a truck going out of service. It is essentially a message form; the Resources Unit uses it to keep its resource status board or T-cards up to date. Operations can also use it as a worksheet.

- **Usually prepared by:** radio or telephone operators in the Communications Unit, as reports come in from resources, Strike Teams, Task Forces, Division/Group Supervisors, Staging Area Managers and air bases.
- **Who gets it:** kept by the Communications Unit and copied to the Resources Unit. The Documentation Unit files it.

General steps for saving, signing and printing are in [Working with ICS forms](help:forms/working_with_forms).

## What goes in each block

| Block | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Incident Name, Operational Period | Filled in for you. |
| Resource Number | The resource's identifier, as assigned by the sending agency or the incident. |
| New Status | **Available** (ready for use now), **Assigned** (checked in and working on a task) or **Out of Service** (on the incident but unable to work). For Out of Service, say why and when it should be back, for example "OS - Mech, ETR 1600"; common reasons are mechanical, rest (off shift) and personnel. |
| From | Where the resource is now and its current status. Name the specific Division, Staging Area or camp when there is more than one. |
| To | The location and status it is moving to. |
| Date / Time | When the change happens (24-hour clock). |
| Comments | Anything else from the resource or dispatch, such as why it is out of service, or the IDs of the units in a Strike Team or Task Force. |
| Prepared By | Your name and position, and when you prepared it. |

Press **Add Status Change Row** to log more changes on the same form.
