# ICS 206 - Medical Plan

The ICS 206 tells responders where to get medical help: the incident's medical aid stations, ambulance services, nearby hospitals, and what to do in a medical emergency. It is part of the Incident Action Plan (IAP).

- **Usually prepared by:** the Medical Unit Leader. The Safety Officer checks it. If aircraft may be used for rescue, coordinate with Air Operations.
- **Who gets it:** everyone who receives the IAP. Medical aid stations and emergency procedures can also be noted on the [ICS 204](help:forms/ics204/index). The signed original goes to the Documentation Unit.

General steps for saving, signing and printing are in [Working with ICS forms](help:forms/working_with_forms).

## What goes in each block

| Block | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Incident Name, Operational Period | Filled in for you. |
| Date/Time Prepared | When the plan was written. The plan may stay the same for the whole incident. |
| 4. Incident Medical Aid Station | One row per aid station: its name, where it is (for example the staging area), a contact number or frequency, and whether paramedics are on site. |
| 5. Transportation | One row per ambulance service: its name, where it is based, contact number or frequency, and level of service (ALS - advanced life support, or BLS - basic life support). Note whether it is air or ground. |
| 6. Hospitals | One row per hospital that could take patients: name, address (and latitude/longitude if it has a helipad), travel time by air and by ground, contact number or frequency, and whether it has a helipad and a burn centre. |
| 7. Special Medical Emergency Procedures | How to report a medical emergency, who to contact and how, and who takes charge of a rescue or injury inside the incident. |
| 8. Prepared By | Normally the Medical Unit Leader. |

## Tips

- Check hospital capabilities and travel times again each period. Road closures and hospital diversions change them.
- The Safety Officer should review the plan before it goes in the IAP. Name them as the approver if your agency does it that way; the default approver is the Logistics Section Chief.
