# ICS 205A - Communications List

The ICS 205A is the incident's contact directory. Where the [ICS 205](help:forms/ics205/index) covers radio channels, the 205A lists how to reach each person: phone, cell, email, pager, satellite phone and so on. It is an optional part of the Incident Action Plan (IAP) and is used alongside the 205.

- **Usually prepared by:** the Communications Unit. Contact details are often collected at check-in. Update it every operational period.
- **Who gets it:** people in the incident organization who need it. The signed original goes to the Documentation Unit.

General steps for signing and printing are in [Working with ICS forms](help:forms/working_with_forms).

## Adding contacts

Under **Add Communications Contact**, enter:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Contact Name | The person's name. |
| Position Title | Their assigned ICS position. |
| Phone, Email | How to reach them. Include the area code. |
| Notes | Other ways to reach them, such as a radio call sign, pager or satellite phone, or the hours they can be reached. |

Saved contacts show under **Existing Entries**. The form stays editable while it is a draft; sign it when it's ready for the period handover or the IAP.

## Handle it as sensitive

This list usually holds personal cell numbers. Don't post it on information boards, put it in documents for the general incident population, or release it to the public. Mark printed copies as containing personal information.

## Tips

- List people alphabetically by position or name, whichever your team finds quicker to scan.
- The [ICS 202](help:forms/ics202/index) treats the 205A as present once it has at least one contact.
