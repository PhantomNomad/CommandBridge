# ICS 215A - Incident Action Plan Safety Analysis

The ICS 215A helps the Safety Officer assess the risks in the planned work: for each area of the incident, what the hazards are and how they will be controlled. It links planning and operations, and is most useful while the plan is being built and for the Operations briefing. It can be posted as a wall chart and can be part of the Incident Action Plan (IAP).

- **Usually prepared by:** the Safety Officer, working with the Operations Section Chief while Operations prepares for the tactics meeting. It goes hand in hand with the operational planning worksheet.
- **Who gets it:** the Resources Unit, to help prepare the Operations briefing. The signed original goes to the Documentation Unit.

General steps for saving, signing and printing are in [Working with ICS forms](help:forms/working_with_forms).

## What goes in each block

| Block | What to enter |
|---|---|
| 1. Incident Name | Filled in for you. |
| 2. Date/Time Prepared | When the analysis was done. |
| 3. Operational Period | Filled in from the active operational period. |
| 4. Incident Area | Each area where people or equipment are likely to meet risks. This is often a Division or Group. One row per area; press **Add Risk Analysis Row** for more. |
| 5. Hazards / Risks | The hazards and risks people are likely to meet in that area while doing the assigned work. |
| 6. Mitigations | What will be done to reduce each risk: protective equipment, a buddy system, escape routes, lookouts and so on. |
| 7. Prepared / Reviewed By | The Safety Officer and the Operations Section Chief, who should prepare it together, with the date and time each reviewed it. Their names are filled in from the current position assignments. |

Only the Incident Commander, the Safety Officer or the Operations Section Chief can fill in Block 7. Everyone else can view the form.

## Tips

- Make sure the people working in each area are briefed on its hazards and mitigations. The [ICS 204](help:forms/ics204/index) Special Instructions block is a good place to repeat the key ones.
- The general safety message for everyone goes on the [ICS 208](help:forms/ics208/index).
- For a large incident, consider a separate analysis for each Branch.
