# ICS 201 - Incident Briefing

The ICS 201 is the first record of an incident. The first Incident Commander uses it to brief whoever takes over command, and to brief the Command and General Staff. It covers the situation, the objectives, what is being done about them, who is in charge of what, and which resources have been ordered. Until a full Incident Action Plan (IAP) exists, the ICS 201 can serve as the first one.

- **Usually prepared by:** the Incident Commander who started the response. In Command Bridge the Planning Section Chief and Documentation Unit Leader can also fill it in when the IC is busy, but only the IC can sign it.
- **Who gets it:** the incoming IC and the Command and General Staff at the first briefing. The map, situation and actions parts help the Situation Unit; the organization and resource parts help the Resources Unit. The signed original goes to the Documentation Unit.

General steps for saving, signing and printing are in [Working with ICS forms](help:forms/working_with_forms).

## What goes in each part

| Part | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Date / Time Prepared | When the briefing was put together. Use **Pick** or type it (24-hour clock). |
| Prepared By | Your name and ICS position. Filled in from your account; correct it if needed. |
| 1. Situation Summary | A short picture of what has happened, what is affected or threatened, and current conditions. Write it so someone arriving cold understands it in a minute. |
| 2. Health and Safety Briefing | The hazards responders face and what is being done about each one: removing the hazard, protective equipment, warning people, keeping people back. |
| 3. Current Objectives | What the response is trying to achieve right now. Add any problem areas. |
| 4. Planned Objectives | What it will try to achieve next. |
| 5. Current Actions, Strategies and Tactics | One row per action: the time it happened or will happen (for example 1430, or TBD) and what was done. Press **Add Current Action Row** for more. |
| 6. Planned Actions, Strategies and Tactics | The same for actions that are planned but not started. |
| 7. Current Organization | Who holds each position: IC (or each Unified Commander and their agency), Command Staff, Section Chiefs and any branches or groups in use. List only positions that are filled. |
| 8. Resource Summary | One row per resource ordered: what was ordered (how many and what kind or type), its identifier or call sign, ETA, whether it is **On Scene**, and where it is or what it is doing. |
| 9. Attachments | Maps, sketches, photos or other documents. See below. |

## Map or sketch

The official form has a space for a map or sketch of the incident: the perimeter or affected area, threatened areas, incident facilities, resource assignments and anything else that helps people picture it. In Command Bridge, upload the map under **Attachments** (BMP, JPG, PNG, PDF or TXT, up to 15 MB each). Attachments are printed after the form in the PDF. If you mark up a map, use standard ICS map symbols.

## Tips

- Keep it current. The 201 is often the only written record of the first hours, so update it before each briefing rather than starting a new one.
- Exact locations and map coordinates that other agencies need also belong on the [ICS 209](help:forms/ics209/index).
- Once the Planning cycle starts, the objectives move to the [ICS 202](help:forms/ics202/index), the organization to the [ICS 203](help:forms/ics203/index) and assignments to the [ICS 204](help:forms/ics204/index).
