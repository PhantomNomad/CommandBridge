# ICS 218 - Support Vehicle/Equipment Inventory

The ICS 218 is the list of every support vehicle and piece of equipment on the incident: buses, pickups, rental cars, generators, dozers and so on. The Ground Support Unit uses it to know what is where; the Resources Unit uses it to track resource status.

- **Usually prepared by:** Ground Support Unit staff, as often as the Ground Support Unit Leader asks.
- **Who gets it:** the Resources Unit. Send the first inventory when it is made, and tell the Resources Unit straight away about any later change in status or location.

General steps for signing and printing are in [Working with ICS forms](help:forms/working_with_forms).

## Adding equipment

Press **Add Equipment** and enter:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Equipment description | What it is, for example "Generator, 20 kW" or "Pickup 4x4". |
| Kind, Type | The resource kind and type from your resource catalogue. |
| Owner/Agency | The agency or company that owns it. |
| Operator name, Contact info | Who runs it and how to reach them (cell phone, radio and so on). |
| Location | Where it is on the incident and what it is assigned to. |
| Order/request number | The order or request number from the agency that sent it, or from the [ICS 213RR](help:forms/ics213rr_v2/index). |
| Status | Received, Available, Assigned, Out of service, Demob pending or Released. |
| Remarks | Features worth knowing (four-wheel drive, towing, high clearance, GPS tracking), the licence plate or serial number, and anything else. |

Group like items together where you can. On paper ICS Canada suggests a separate sheet for each category, such as buses or generators.

## Where entries come from

When the [Resources Unit](help:forms/resources/index) receives a vehicle or piece of equipment ordered on an ICS 213RR, **Add to ICS 218** creates the entry for you. People and crews go on the [ICS 211](help:forms/ics211/index) instead. Equipment on this list is counted in the resource summary of the [ICS 209](help:forms/ics209/index); items marked Released are left out.

## Printing

- **219 Card** prints an ICS 219-style resource status card (T-card) for one item, for a T-card rack.
- **Preview PDF** prints the current inventory on the official ICS 218. **Download Blank Form** gives you the empty form.
