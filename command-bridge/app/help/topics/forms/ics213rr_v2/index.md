# ICS 213RR - Resource Request

The ICS 213RR is how anyone on the incident asks for a resource: people, equipment, supplies or services. Command Bridge follows each request from the person who needs it, through approval, Logistics ordering it, arrival and check-in, and Finance review, until it is closed and filed.

The ICS Canada Forms Catalogue (2022) doesn't include the 213RR, so this page describes how Command Bridge handles it. Follow your agency's ordering procedures for spending limits and who may approve what.

## The steps

The request page shows which step it is at and, under **Next action**, what the next person has to do.

| Step | Status | Who acts |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Request | Draft (or Returned for revision) | The requester fills it in and presses **Submit request**. |
| 2. Approval | Awaiting approval | The approving authority confirms the need and presses **Approve & send to Logistics**. |
| 3. Logistics | Awaiting Logistics | Logistics places the order and presses **Complete Logistics**. |
| 4. Arrival | Awaiting arrival / receipt, Partially fulfilled, Finance review | The resource arrives and is received or checked in. Finance reviews the cost at the same time. |
| 5. Done | Fulfilled, then Closed | Someone authorized presses **Close & file request**. |

Who approves at step 2 depends on the requester's position:

- Requests from the **Incident Commander or a Section Chief** (Planning, Logistics, Finance/Admin) skip approval and go straight to Logistics.
- Requests from the **Command Staff** (Liaison, Safety, Information Officer) go to the Incident Commander.
- Everyone else's requests go to their Section Chief.

Each step sends a dashboard notification to the people who act next.

## Filling in the request

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Priority | Urgent, Routine or Low. |
| Requested by | Your name and position. Filled in from your account. |
| Quantity, Kind, Type | How many, and what kind and type from the resource catalogue. |
| Detailed item description | Enough detail that Logistics orders the right thing: size, capacity, special features, qualifications. |
| Estimated cost | Your best estimate, if known. |
| Needed by | When it must be there. |
| Requested delivery/reporting location | Where it should be delivered or report. |
| Suitable substitutes | What else would do if the exact item isn't available. |
| Suggested sources / remarks | Any supplier you know of, and anything else Logistics should know. |

You don't decide whether the Resources Unit tracks the resource; Logistics or the Resources Unit Leader does. Once submitted, the request can't be changed. To change it, have it returned for revision, or cancel it and make a new one.

## Logistics

Logistics records the order number, who placed the order (for example the Logistics Section Chief or Supply Unit Leader), the supplier and their contact, and notes. It also chooses how the resource is tracked:

- **Track through Resources Unit**: people, crews, vehicles and equipment. An expected check-in record goes to the [Resources Unit](help:forms/resources/index) queue for the receiving location you pick.
- **No tracking required**: supplies, consumables and services. The receiver records delivery on the request with **Record receipt / fulfillment** (full or partial).
- **RESL to determine**: the Resources Unit Leader decides.

There must be at least one active location set up in [Locations](help:admin/locations) before Logistics can be completed. If Logistics can't fill the request, it is marked **Unable to fill**.

## Finance

Finance/Administration is notified when Logistics completes the order and reviews it on the [Finance/Admin 213RR queue](help:forms/finance/index). Finance review runs alongside delivery and does not hold up receiving or check-in.

## Cancelling

**Cancel request** stops a request that is no longer needed. Give a reason. A resource that has already been received or checked in should be released through demobilization instead.

## The request list

**Open requests** lists every active request with its current step, status, requester and last update. Closed and cancelled requests are listed separately. Each request has an **Activity timeline** showing every step, who did it and when. **Preview PDF** prints it on the official form.
