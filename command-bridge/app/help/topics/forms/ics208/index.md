# ICS 208 - Safety Message/Plan

The ICS 208 expands on the safety message: the known hazards for the operational period, the precautions people must take, and whether a site safety plan is needed. In Command Bridge it is one of the forms the [ICS 202](help:forms/ics202/index) needs before the Incident Action Plan (IAP) can be approved.

- **Usually prepared by:** the Safety Officer.
- **Who gets it:** everyone who receives the IAP. The signed original goes to the Documentation Unit.

General steps for saving, signing and printing are in [Working with ICS forms](help:forms/working_with_forms).

## What goes in each block

| Block | What to enter |
|---|---|
| 1. Incident Name | Filled in for you. |
| 2. Operational Period | Filled in from the active operational period. |
| 3. Safety Message / Plan | Short, clear safety messages, priorities and key command decisions on safety: known hazards and the precautions to take during this period. Refer to and attach any further safety messages. |
| Site Safety Plan Required | Tick if a site safety plan is needed for this incident. |
| Site Safety Plan Location | Where the approved site safety plan(s) can be found. |
| 4. Prepared By / Safety Officer | The Safety Officer's name and position. |

## Tips

- Keep the general safety message on the 202 (Block 5) in line with this form.
- The hazard-by-area analysis belongs on the [ICS 215A](help:forms/ics215a/index). The 208 is the message everyone reads.
- If the message runs past the box, the full text prints on a continuation sheet.
