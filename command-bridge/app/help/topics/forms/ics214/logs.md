# ICS 214 - Activity Logs (everyone)

A list of the ICS 214 activity logs you are allowed to open for the selected operational period. Change the period from the top bar to see earlier logs.

- **Incident Commander / Unified Command** and **SysAdmins** see everyone.
- **Supervisors** see the people who report to their position.
- The **Documentation Unit Leader** sees everyone, read only.

Each row shows the person, their position(s), the log's status, how many entries have been typed in, and when it was last changed. "Not started" means the person has no log for this period yet; it starts when they open it or someone adds an entry.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Open | Taking entries |
| Returned | Sent back for changes; taking entries again |
| Signed | Signed by the person, waiting for approval; locked |
| Approved | Approved by the supervisor; locked |

See [ICS 214 - Activity Log](help:forms/ics214/index) for how a log works.
