# ICS 214 - Activity Log

Every person has their **own** ICS 214 for each operational period. It is started when your login is created (or the first time you open it), so it shows when your account was made, your sign-ins and sign-outs, and everything you do after that.

In ICS, the activity log records the notable things that happen at any level: decisions, assignments, contacts, briefings, status changes. It gives the incident a lasting record for handovers, after-action reviews and cost recovery. The signed logs go to the Documentation Unit.

General steps for signing and printing are in [Working with ICS forms](help:forms/working_with_forms).

## What is recorded automatically

- Your sign-ins and sign-outs, and changes to your account.
- Forms you save, sign, submit or approve.
- Meetings marked **Completed** on the [ICS 230](help:forms/ics230/index) ("Meeting held: ..."). These appear on everyone's log. A meeting that is still Scheduled, Tentative or Cancelled is not shown.

Use the **Show** check boxes to hide groups of lines while you read.

## Adding an entry

Type what happened in **Notable activity** and press **Add entry**. Leave Date/Time blank to use the current time, or pick the time it happened.

Entries can't be changed or deleted, by anyone. To correct one, add a new entry such as "The 14:20 entry was in error: the truck went to Staging B."

## Who can open your log

| Who | Can do |
|---|---|
| You | Read, add entries, edit the header (name, position, agency, unit), sign it |
| Incident Commander / Unified Command | Read, add entries, ask you to update it |
| SysAdmin | Read, add entries, ask you to update it |
| Your supervisor (the position you report to) | Read, add entries, ask you to update it |
| Documentation Unit Leader | Read only, and ask you to update it |

Entries added by someone else show their name in the **Source** column. Nobody else can open your log.

The IC, SysAdmins, supervisors and the DOCL can open **Everyone's activity logs** for a list of the logs they may open.

## Signing and locking

When the period is over, press **Sign and submit ICS 214** in the Sign-off box. Only you can sign your own log. Your supervisor is chosen as the approver.

- After you sign, the log is locked: no more entries or header changes.
- While it is waiting for approval, you or the approver can **Return for changes**, which opens it again.
- Once it is **approved** it stays locked. Only a SysAdmin can reopen an approved log.

If someone asks you to update your log, you get a notification. If the log is already signed, the approver has to return it first.

## Printing

**Preview PDF** and **Download PDF** print the log you are looking at, for that person and operational period.
