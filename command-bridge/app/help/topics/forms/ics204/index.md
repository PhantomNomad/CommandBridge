# ICS 204 - Assignment List

The ICS 204 tells a Division or Group supervisor what their part of the incident is doing this operational period: who is in charge, which resources they have, what work they are to do, special instructions and how to communicate. It is part of the Incident Action Plan (IAP).

- **Usually prepared by:** the Resources Unit, using the [ICS 202](help:forms/ics202/index) objectives, the operational planning worksheet and direction from the Operations Section Chief.
- **Approved by:** ICS Canada says the Incident Commander approves it, ideally after the Planning and Operations Section Chiefs have reviewed it. In Command Bridge the Planning Section Chief approves it (see below).
- **Who gets it:** everyone who receives the IAP, especially the Division or Group named on it. The approved original goes to the Documentation Unit.

General steps for saving and printing are in [Working with ICS forms](help:forms/working_with_forms).

## What goes in each block

| Block | What to enter |
|---|---|
| 1. Branch | In a large incident, the Branch number or name, for reference. Leave blank if branches aren't used. |
| 2. Division/Group/Staging | The Division letter (A, B, C ...) or the Group's functional name, or the staging area. Pick from the list or type your own. |
| 3. Incident Name | Filled in from the incident. |
| 4. Operational Period | Filled in from the active operational period. |
| 5. Operational Personnel | Names and contact numbers for the Operations Section Chief, the Branch Director, the Division/Group Supervisor and the Staging Area Manager, as they apply. |
| 6. Resources Assigned to this Period | One row per resource: its identifier (use TBD if it's ordered but not known yet), the leader's name, how many people including the leader, how to reach the leader (include the area code for phones), and notes such as where and when to report, special equipment, whether they've been briefed, and transport needs. Press **Add Resource** for more rows. |
| 7. Work Assignments | What this Division or Group must get done this period. |
| 8. Special Instructions | Safety problems, precautions, drop-off and pick-up points and anything else they must know. |
| 9. Division/Group Communications Summary | The radio channels (function, frequency, system and channel from the [ICS 205](help:forms/ics205/index)) and phone numbers this group needs, including emergency numbers. Put full details on the 205 and [205A](help:forms/ics205a/index). The 204 is widely shared, so think twice before listing personal cell numbers. |
| Prepared By | Your name and ICS position. |

## Submitting and approval

1. Save as you go.
2. The Incident Commander, a SysAdmin or the Resources Unit Leader presses **Submit**.
3. The Planning Section Chief gets a dashboard notification and presses **Approve ICS 204 (Planning Section Chief)**. Until then the form shows "Waiting for Planning Section Chief approval".

> Command Bridge currently keeps **one** ICS 204 for the incident. If you have several Divisions or Groups, list them in Block 2 and separate their resources and assignments clearly, or attach extra pages, until separate 204s per Division are supported.
