# Audit Log

A read-only record of who did what and when: sign-ins and sign-outs, account changes, forms saved, signed and approved, settings changes and other important actions. Nothing in it can be edited or deleted.

**Who can open it:** SysAdmins and anyone holding Incident Commander or Unified Command in the active incident. It is in the left menu (and under Settings for administrators).

## Filters

- **Person** shows everything that person did, plus anything done to their account (for example "SysAdmin reset the PIN of ...").
- **Category**: Sign-ins, Accounts, Forms, or Settings / other. These are the same groups used on the ICS 214.
- **From / To** limit the dates (both days included).
- **Contains** searches the description, the action and the person's name.
- **Incidents** (SysAdmins only): this incident, or all incidents. "This incident" also includes events that don't belong to any incident, such as sign-ins.
- **Show** 25, 50, 100, 250 or All rows per page. "All" shows up to 20,000 rows; narrow the filters for more.

Newest entries are first. Hover over a description to see the raw action name. There is no export.
