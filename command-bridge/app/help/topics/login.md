# Signing in

How you sign in depends on your account:

- **Technical Services Coordinator (TSC) administrators** sign in with their username and **password**.
- **Incident users** sign in with their username and **PIN**.
- If your organization has turned on Microsoft sign-in, use the **Sign in with Microsoft** button instead.

After several wrong attempts the account, or sign-ins from your network, are paused for a while. Ask your TSC or
Incident Commander to unlock your account if you cannot wait.

You are signed out automatically after 60 minutes without activity. Anything not saved on the page you were using is lost.
